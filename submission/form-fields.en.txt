--- DESCRIPTION START ---
Blueprint + Cavity makes AI answers inspectable through two parallel cases: construction measurement and nanoscale light cavities. Our goal is to expose the decisions behind an answer and provide feedback that reflects meaningful progress.

**Blueprint: an environment for learning from real mistakes**
An agent receives a construction drawing and a work order. It can inspect PDFs and images, render crops, use OCR and geometry tools, calculate quantities, and submit a value, unit, confidence, evidence regions and assumptions. The challenge is spatial judgment: choosing the correct room boundary, recovering scale and deducting openings.

We built 36 Harbor-compatible tasks across 12 scenarios and three document conditions: vector PDF, clean image and synthetic scan. The offline reward combines numerical correctness (75%), supporting evidence (15%) and confidence calibration (10%). Evidence credit depends on the numerical result. A no-op earns zero; valid abstention earns at most 0.1.

The historical evaluation contains 144 trials: 36 Kimi, 36 DeepSeek, 36 privileged gold-reference oracle and 36 no-op. The 72 model attempts include saved tool replays. Mean rewards are 0.8073 for Kimi and 0.3924 for DeepSeek, versus 1.0 for the oracle and 0.0 for no-op. These are rewards, not accuracy percentages. In one room-area task, DeepSeek reports 29.1 m² and Kimi 26.4 m² against a 26.9 m² reference; the replay reveals a boundary assumption behind the error. Visitors can inspect that reasoning, measure the drawing themselves and use the same scorer.

**Cavity: making scientific claims understandable**
Hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) is a layered crystal where infrared light couples to atomic vibrations. Cavity geometry changes the resulting fields and resonances. Our interactive explorer presents precomputed RCWA/COMSOL fields, modes and spectra so visitors can see the physics before assessing a numerical claim.

Changing the analysis window on the same saved spectrum changes the estimated quality factor Q from 52.7 to 96.4. This demonstrates sensitivity to analysis choices, not an improved device. A companion browser-local checker compares a supported numeric answer pasted from an AI reply with a fixed mathematical reference; it does not validate a proof or cavity design. Cavity is a parallel research case, separate from the construction benchmark; it runs no new solver or live model call.

**Built for inspection and reproduction**
The password-free website includes guided interactions, a results ledger, model replays, an English-captioned film and a detailed Chinese manual. The public repository includes tasks, scoring code, documented sources and 240 deterministic verifier checks. These checks test scoring behavior; they are not additional model runs.

The stack combines Python/FastAPI, a browser-based interface, Harbor/OpenCode, Verda inference and Modal execution for the recorded evaluations. Each model/task pair has one recorded formal attempt. The oracle has privileged answers; three independent repeats and model-weight training have not been performed. Shared drawing sources do not establish unseen-building generalization. A separate command prepares fresh judge runs; execution requires provider credentials and its runtime network policy remains unverified.
