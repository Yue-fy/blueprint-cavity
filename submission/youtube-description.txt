WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS
Blueprint has 36 construction tasks across five task families and three drawing conditions. Its 144 historical trials comprise 72 actual model trials with saved replays, 36 privileged gold-reference oracle trials and 36 no-op trials. Mean verifier rewards: Kimi 0.807308, DeepSeek 0.392409, oracle 1.000000, no-op 0.000000. These are rewards, not accuracy percentages. The oracle is a privileged reference, not a fair same-input model baseline.

Cavity explores precomputed hBN nanocavity fields, modes, spectra and linewidth-window sensitivity. Pasted AI replies are checked locally for numerical consistency. It is not a live AI API, an online scientific agent or a new simulation run. Cavity has a different evidence scope from the construction benchmark.

The film contains recorded model replays and human UI interaction. It does not show live inference. Three independent agent repeats have not been run, and no model weights have been trained.

English captions are burned into the film; a separate English subtitle track is provided for upload. Music only, with no narration.
