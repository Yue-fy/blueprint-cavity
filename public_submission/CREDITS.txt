PROJECT DISPLAY AND EVIDENCE

Blueprint + Cavity presents the project's interface recordings, historical model replays, human interaction and owner-authorized precomputed hBN visualizations. The owner authorized public display of the existing complete demo and its embedded hBN data. That display authorization is not a new blanket open-data license for research materials.

The field visualizations are computed scientific displays, not microscopy photographs. Historical recorded model actions are not live inference. The Cavity browser checker validates supported numerical fields from pasted text; it does not prove the science or call a model provider.
