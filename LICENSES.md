The USACE classification is based on the selected sheets' explicit federal author/title blocks, their official DoD standard-design publication, and absence of a contractor credit or conflicting copyright notice on those sheets. It is an authorship-based determination, not a bespoke written permission from USACE. As [USAGov explains](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright), government hosting alone does not establish public-domain status, and status can differ outside the United States. Government names and marks remain part of the source drawing; this project does not claim government endorsement. No AFCEC/AFCEE contractor-authored drawing is included.

`data/sources.json` preserves the downloaded-publication SHA-256, original neutral-sheet SHA-256, normalized bundled-sheet SHA-256, publication page, verification basis and date. Each neutral USACE sheet's extracted text exactly matched the indicated page of a freshly downloaded primary publication. The DfE original also matched the public PDF byte for byte. The neutral originals were read only. All bundled PDFs were rebuilt with rotation normalized and metadata removed; visible source content and physical scale are retained. Attachments, scripts and annotations were not imported.

Clean PNGs are full-page renders, with the longest side 4096 pixels. Synthetic scans apply grayscale blur, contrast reduction, seeded noise and JPEG compression, then save PNGs. There is no crop, skew, rotation, or subsequent size change. Exact seeds, dimensions, transformations and output hashes are recorded in `data/sources.json`; these are simulated degradations, not real scanned documents.

# Annotation provenance and limitations

The captain authorized use of the named development manifests and human expert annotations on 14 November 2026; see `data/authorization.json` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Gold values retain their original reference IDs and provenance. Human expert methods are distinguished from agent-generated development references. Newly created evidence boxes are attributed to the AfterQuery curation agent, never to the human expert. Public catalogues and agent images contain neither gold values nor these private annotation records.

The selected set contains 12 scenarios × 3 input conditions = 36 tasks: area 6, skirting 9, openings 6, slab 6, piping 9. Every family is balanced across vector, clean and synthetic-scan conditions. Three tasks, the condition variants of the D13 slab scenario, test confusing a printed slab elevation with an area. Other tasks also involve scope and measurement conventions but are not tagged as traps.

Nine held-out tasks belong to scenarios `bp-02`, `bp-06`, and `bp-11`; all three condition variants stay together. Holdout scenarios share drawings with the training split. Every scenario was inspected during curation. This is a reserved scenario split for subsequent prompt/policy evaluation, not an untouched blind test or evidence of generalization to unseen buildings. The small three-drawing collection is intended as a verifiable environment demonstration.

Skirting values use the expert's stated conventions: exclude door openings and jamb reveals, treat serving hatches as having wall below, and omit the hall's internal folding partition. Laundry accepts both the expert's 9.96 m and the independently measured development reference of 10.08 m. Whole-plan door totals with ±3 uncertainty were excluded. The previously recorded rectangular kitchen area was excluded because the expert identified an L-shaped room. The D16 task explicitly asks for the six-panel rectangular field, excluding its projecting stair bay; the D13 task asks for gross slab plan area before trench deductions.

Evidence annotations identify rooms, wall openings, tags and slab regions. Region boxes identify the relevant scope; they do not independently prove every perimeter segment or dimension. West-facade window spans were independently enumerated from vector glazing geometry and checked against the expert's 18-opening count; the entrance's two sliding doors were visually inspected. CV and FP evidence uses exact word matches to avoid confusing CV tags with FSCV tags. No claim is made that all values received a new professional survey or that synthetic variants were separately annotated by an expert.
